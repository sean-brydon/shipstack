**What it is.** Every skill in the stack. You can type any of them; `/shipit` calls most of them for you.

**Why.** Each one does one job well, so a playbook can sequence them and you can reach for one directly.

## Setup and help

| skill | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| [/shipit](/skills/shipit) | Routes a task to the playbook for its kind, runs it with subagents and the other skills, and stops only when there is proof. | varies |
| [/setup-shipit](/skills/setup-shipit) | Picks the model per role and the effort budget, and detects codex and berth. | light |
| [/shipit-help](/skills/shipit-help) | Tells you which skill or playbook fits, and hands you a prompt to send. | light |

## Understand

| skill | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| [/how](/skills/how) | Explains how a subsystem works at runtime: the pieces, the flow, where things live, the gotchas. | medium |
| [/why](/skills/why) | Finds why something is the way it is, from every source it can reach: git history, tickets, docs, chat, logs, error tracking, analytics. | medium |
| [/teach](/skills/teach) | Runs how and why together and weaves them into one plain explanation, built up diagram by diagram. | heavy |
| [/recall](/skills/recall) | Rebuilds your recent context on a topic from your own transcripts, project memory, live state and past fixes. | light |
| [/ketchup](/skills/ketchup) | Catches you up on what changed in this chat since your last message. | light |
| [/in-your-own-words](/skills/in-your-own-words) | Plays back your goal and the real problem before any work starts. | light |
| [/sharpen](/skills/sharpen) | Reframes a task the way a top expert would approach it, aimed at what you're after, without over-specifying. | light |
| [/bro](/skills/bro) | Restates the last reply in plain words, no jargon. | light |
| [/grill](/skills/grill) | Interviews you in rounds until the plan is sharp: numbered questions, a recommended answer for each, facts looked up instead of asked. Records settled terms in GLOSSARY.md and decisions in docs/adr/. | light |

## Design and review

| skill | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| [/architect](/skills/architect) | Settles types, signatures and module shape before code, with parallel designs, then stays in the loop while code fills in. | medium |
| [/arena](/skills/arena) | Runs several attempts at the same thing in separate worktrees, picks the best as a base, and grafts in the best parts of the others. | heavy |
| [/swarm](/skills/swarm) | Fans out workers across slices or races, five at a time, and returns one report with a coverage table. | heavy |
| [/interrogate](/skills/interrogate) | Reviewers on different model families attack a diff from separate angles, then a lead sorts real findings from noise. | heavy |
| [/blast-radius](/skills/blast-radius) | Finds what a small-looking change could break elsewhere, and proves the one fact it's safe because of by running code. | medium |
