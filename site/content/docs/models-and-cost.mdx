**What it does.** Runs each subagent on the model its role names, at an effort set by your budget, and prints what the run cost.

**Why.** Reading is cheap and judgment isn't. Matching the model to the role cuts cost without cutting quality, and the receipt shows the real number.

| role | default |
|---|---|
| code delegates (feature, refactoring, bug fix, perf, hillclimb, swarm workers) | sonnet |
| verification | sonnet |
| judgment, prose, hardest tasks, how explainer, why synthesizer | opus |
| reading: how explorers, why investigators, recall and source readers | haiku |
| `/interrogate` reviewers, `/arena` runners | opus, sonnet, fable |
| `/architect` runners | opus, sonnet |

A role also takes `inherit` (your session's model) or `codex` (when it is on your `PATH`). `/setup-shipit` writes your choices to `~/.claude/shipit/models.md`.

**Budget.** It sets the starting effort: `unlimited` is `max`, `large` (default) `xhigh`, `medium` `high`, `small` `medium`. The hardest work goes one step up, routine work one or more down; reading roles stay at the start.

## The eval

Three `/how` questions on one Go codebase, 9 runs, scored blind by one Opus judge. Haiku `xhigh` scored highest for about 1/15 of Sonnet's cost, so reading roles default to Haiku. The cost gap is the robust part; the quality gap could flip on other questions.

**Cost receipt.** `python3 -I ~/.claude/skills/shipit/adapters/claude-code/scripts/cost.py` prints the session's total by agent and by model at list API prices (`--session <id>`, `--csv out.csv`, `--all-agents`). Output tokens are a floor, and on a subscription the dollars are usage, not a bill.
