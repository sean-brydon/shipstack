shipstack adapts [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) by Lauren Tan (MIT), ported from Cursor to Claude Code at `cursor/plugins@1e5c90a4`. The playbooks, the 24 principles, the review panels and the writing rules are pstack's.

shipstack adds Claude model roles, berth, the proof pack, auto-mode handling, and the Ideation, Research and Design audit playbooks. It is MIT licensed; see [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/sean-brydon/shipstack/blob/main/LICENSE).
