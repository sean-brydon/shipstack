## What to notice

- **Repro first.** Both runs printed the invoice and did the arithmetic (899.55 cents) before touching code, which ruled out a float error.
- **A failing test, then the fix.** The test in its own commit, then `Math.floor` to `Math.round` on top. No subagents: $0.40 and under a minute each.
- **No todo list.** Headless `claude -p` has no todo tool, so the skipped steps are listed in the reply, each with a reason.
