**The rule.** Build 2-3 competing prototypes and compare before committing.

<details className="ss-full">
<summary>Full instructions (what the agent reads)</summary>

**Applies when:** you face a novel UI interaction or architectural decision with no precedent in the codebase.

Build 2-3 competing prototypes and compare them side by side before committing.

Build each prototype in its own worktree, with a writing helper per prototype (see your adapter). Compare what each one does, not what its author says it does. For UI, drive the real surface and put screenshots of each side by side.

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.

</details>
